# Product scope

kinCore makes the household plan visible and gives people credit for doing the work. It is built for a shared kitchen screen and the phones people already carry.

| Area | Available at this snapshot | Practical boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Household access | Invitations, memberships, roles, profile selection, returning-account sessions | A profile is not a separate login credential. |
| Chores | Presets, custom tasks, assignment, recurrence, overdue state and late-point rules | Open repeats do not accumulate while previous work is unfinished. |
| Routines | Named groups and ordered checklists | A group organizes chores; each chore retains its own schedule and award. |
| Progress | Points, levels, streaks, hundreds of achievements, secret reveals | Catalog breadth does not imply every future rule type is implemented. |
| Insights | 30/90/365-day and all-time totals, period comparisons, activity, category effort, on-time results, personal records | All-time graphs show up to 365 recent days. Contribution is not a fairness score. |
| Calendar | Scheduled chores and selected read-only Google events; month/week/agenda | Google edits and invitation responses remain in Google; public verification pending. |
| Notes | Shared notes, pinning, reactions, expiry and archive | No claim of email reminders or AI assistants. |
| Friends | Opt-in household connections and aggregate comparisons | Private chore titles, notes and calendars are excluded. |
| Shared screens | Pairing, revocation, touch-oriented Fridge Mode, queued completions | Physical hardware compatibility still requires device testing. |
| Support | Household cosmetics, supporter recognition, Legend PDF keepsakes | No extra points, easier achievements, or core feature paywall. |

## The mission is part of the product

Core household features, profiles, gameplay, and existing JSON/CSV exports remain free. Optional Supporter ($3+ USD/month) and Legend ($6+ USD/month) support the service. Legend includes formatted monthly/yearly PDF keepsakes. A standard Graphite dark mode is available to everyone.

## Planned, not available

- kinBuddy avatar outfits, gear, collectible rewards, and community events are concepts.
- General realtime subscriptions and independent self-hosting remain future work.
- Additional chore metadata and deeper achievement rule families remain on the original roadmap.
- Public Google OAuth approval, physical device acceptance, broader accessibility/reconnect testing, and operational validation remain launch work.

There are no claims of universal fridge compatibility, native mobile apps, Apple Calendar sync, automatic cleaning detection, guaranteed relationship outcomes, or finished enterprise compliance.

The [public interactive preview](https://kincore.co/welcome#preview) uses fictional data. The local demo in this repository is a separate, smaller engineering illustration.
